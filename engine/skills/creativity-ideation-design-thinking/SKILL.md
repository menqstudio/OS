# Creativity Ideation Design Thinking

## Trigger
Use when the task is to generate, diverge, or structure novel options for an ambiguous problem: naming, concept generation, feature ideation, workshop/design-sprint facilitation, reframing a stuck problem, or moving from user pain to testable concepts. Do NOT use for visual/UI production or brand systems (route to marketing-brand) or for prioritizing an already-defined backlog.

## Inputs
- The problem framed as a "How might we…" question plus the user or job-to-be-done.
- Known constraints (technical, budget, timeline, brand), and explicit non-goals.
- Success signal: what a good idea must achieve and how it will be judged.
- Existing attempts and why they failed (to avoid re-treading dead ends).

## Workflow
1. Reframe: restate the problem as 2-3 competing "How might we…" questions at different altitudes; confirm which one to solve.
2. Empathize: name the user, their job-to-be-done, and the top 3 pains/gains driving the need.
3. Diverge deliberately — generate a large quantity without judgment; use forced techniques (SCAMPER, analogies from other domains, worst-possible-idea inversion, crazy-8s) to escape the obvious.
4. Cluster ideas into themes; label the distinct strategic bets rather than individual sticky notes.
5. Converge: score against the supplied success signal and constraints (desirability, feasibility, viability); pick 2-3 to advance.
6. Sharpen each survivor into a concept: one-line pitch, who it's for, the key assumption it rests on, and the cheapest test to validate that assumption.
7. Define the next experiment (prototype fidelity, what to measure, kill/continue threshold).

## Outputs
- Reframed problem statement(s) with the chosen HMW.
- A broad idea list plus clustered themes.
- 2-3 sharpened concepts, each with pitch, target user, riskiest assumption, and a concrete validation test.
- A prioritization rationale tied to stated criteria.

## Safety limits
No scope expansion, secret access, credential handling, push, merge, deployment, deletion, external communication, or production mutation without the exact governing grant and approval boundary. Do not present unvalidated ideas as proven, invent market data, or commit resources. Ambiguous mutation targets fail closed.

## Handoffs
Concept-to-execution build to the relevant engineering/product role. Brand and visual identity to marketing-brand. Go-to-market of a chosen concept to sales-revenue-growth or partnership-channel-bd. Escalate cross-domain decisions to the owning SST role; medium, high, and critical work requires an independent verifier; release actions hand off only to the authorized executor.

## Verification
Confirm every concept names its target user, riskiest assumption, and a cheap falsifiable test; ideas map back to the chosen HMW and success signal; convergence criteria are stated, not implicit. No fabricated evidence of demand. Concepts asserted as validated without a test remain RED.

## Failure and rollback
Stop on an undefined problem, missing success signal, or pressure to commit build resources without grant. Discard the working set, restore the prior framing, and report the missing input. Never label an untested concept validated or GREEN.
