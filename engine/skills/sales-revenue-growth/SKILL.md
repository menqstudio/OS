# Sales Revenue Growth

## Trigger
Use when the task is pipeline strategy, deal qualification/coaching, discovery and objection handling, forecast review, pricing/proposal structuring, funnel/conversion diagnosis, ICP and territory design, or expansion/renewal playbooks. Do NOT use for consumer marketing copy (route to marketing-brand) or partner-channel economics (route to partnership-channel-bd).

## Inputs
- Deal/pipeline data: stage, amount, close date, champion, and qualification status.
- ICP definition and the specific buyer's role, pain, and buying process.
- Funnel metrics: conversion by stage, cycle length, win rate, ACV, and leakage points.
- Pricing structure, discount authority, and competitive alternatives in the deal.

## Workflow
1. Qualify ruthlessly against a framework (MEDDICC/MEDDPICC or equivalent): Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria/process, Identified pain, Champion, Competition — flag every missing element as deal risk.
2. Diagnose the funnel by data: find the stage with worst conversion × volume and attack that leak first, not the vanity top-of-funnel.
3. Coach discovery: quantify the pain in the buyer's terms (cost of inaction), confirm the economic buyer and decision process, and validate a real champion who sells internally.
4. Handle objections by category (price, timing, authority, need, trust): acknowledge, isolate the real concern, reframe to value/ROI, and confirm resolution — never discount reflexively to close.
5. Structure pricing/proposals to value not cost-plus; use packaging and terms as levers before price; keep discounting tied to reciprocal concessions and within authority.
6. Forecast honestly: categorize commit/best-case/pipeline on evidence (next step scheduled, champion confirmed), not optimism; call slipping deals early.
7. For expansion: map whitespace, tie the renewal/upsell to realized value and product adoption signals, and time the ask to a success moment.

## Outputs
- Deal qualification and risk assessment with the specific gaps to close.
- Funnel diagnosis with the prioritized leak and remediation.
- Discovery/objection playbook and value-based proposal structure.
- Evidence-based forecast categorization and expansion/renewal plan.

## Safety limits
No scope expansion, secret access, credential handling, push, merge, deployment, deletion, external communication, or production mutation without the exact governing grant and approval boundary. Never send outreach, extend pricing/discounts beyond authority, or alter CRM records of record without grant. Do not fabricate pipeline, forecast, or customer commitments. Ambiguous mutation targets fail closed.

## Handoffs
Contract terms and MSA to legal-compliance-contracts. Pay-band-touching compensation/quota design to hr-compensation-design. Partner/channel deals to partnership-channel-bd. Messaging and positioning to marketing-brand. Escalate cross-domain decisions to the owning SST role; medium, high, and critical work requires an independent verifier; outreach/discount/CRM writes hand off only to the authorized executor.

## Verification
Confirm every advancing deal has economic buyer, quantified pain, decision process, and champion evidenced; funnel remediation targets the data-worst stage; discounts stay within authority and tie to reciprocal terms; forecast categories rest on concrete next steps. Any pipeline or commitment claim without evidence remains RED.

## Failure and rollback
Stop on unqualified deals presented as committed, missing ICP, discounting beyond authority, or a request to send/write CRM without grant. Discard the recommendation, restore prior state, and report the missing input. Never mark an unqualified deal or inflated forecast GREEN.
