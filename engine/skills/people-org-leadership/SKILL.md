# People Org Leadership

## Trigger
Use when the task is org design, team topology, role/responsibility clarity (RACI), performance management, feedback/coaching frameworks, hiring plans and scorecards, onboarding/ramp design, or handling a people situation (conflict, underperformance, reorg comms). Do NOT use for pay bands/comp mechanics (route to hr-compensation-design) or binding employment terms (route to legal-compliance-contracts).

## Inputs
- The team/org context: size, mission, current structure, and the problem being solved.
- Roles involved, decision rights, and where accountability is currently ambiguous.
- Performance/feedback data or the specific situation and desired outcome.
- Constraints: headcount, budget, timeline, and any HR/legal boundaries.

## Workflow
1. Diagnose before restructuring: name the actual problem (unclear ownership, span, dependency, capability gap) — reorgs that skip diagnosis recreate the pain.
2. For org design: define the mission and outcomes each team owns, then structure to minimize cross-team dependencies and clarify decision rights (who decides vs. consults vs. informed).
3. For role clarity: build a RACI on the disputed decisions/deliverables; eliminate multiple accountable owners.
4. For performance: separate behavior from outcome; use specific, observable examples; SBI (situation-behavior-impact); define a clear expectation, support plan, and timeline before any escalation.
5. For hiring: write an outcome-based scorecard (mission, key outcomes, competencies) and a structured, evidence-based interview loop with anti-bias controls.
6. Design the people comms: what changes, why, what it means for each person, and what's NOT changing — address the "what about me" question directly.
7. Plan the ramp/onboarding to first meaningful contribution with milestones and an owner.

## Outputs
- Org/team design with mission, ownership, and decision rights.
- RACI or role-clarity map for the disputed area.
- Performance/coaching plan (expectation, evidence, support, timeline) or hiring scorecard + loop.
- Change-communication plan and onboarding/ramp milestones.

## Safety limits
No scope expansion, secret access, credential handling, push, merge, deployment, deletion, external communication, or production mutation without the exact governing grant and approval boundary. Never make or communicate termination/hiring decisions, disclose individual performance data beyond grant, or send org announcements without grant. Treat personnel data as confidential. Do not fabricate performance evidence. Ambiguous mutation targets fail closed.

## Handoffs
Compensation and leveling to hr-compensation-design. Termination/employment legal to legal-compliance-contracts. Sensitive announcements' wording to communication-writing-negotiation. Escalate cross-domain decisions to the owning SST role; medium, high, and critical work requires an independent verifier; any personnel action or announcement hands off only to the authorized executor.

## Verification
Confirm the design traces to a named problem, decision rights have single accountable owners, performance plans cite specific observable evidence with a support path and timeline, hiring scorecards are outcome-based with bias controls, and comms answer "what changes for me." Any performance claim without evidence remains RED.

## Failure and rollback
Stop on an undiagnosed reorg, missing performance evidence, ambiguous authority over a personnel decision, or a request to announce/act without grant. Discard the plan, restore prior state, and report the missing input. Never mark an unverified people decision GREEN.
